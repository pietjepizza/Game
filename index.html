How it works

First time right. Miss one entry, or post a duplicate, and the close fails. Survive all 90 seconds for a 50% flawless bonus. Entries are worth more as the clock runs.

Scoring

  • Streak Post by hand without a pause of more than 1.6 seconds. 10 in a row gives ×1.2, 25 gives ×1.5, 50 gives ×2.
  • Clutch save Post an entry just above the close line for +50% on that entry.
  • Month-end spikes Twice per close, extra volume hits for 6 seconds, with a 3-second warning. Survive a spike for a bonus.
  • Debrief and AI deck Every close earns an AI card for your deck (AI basics, working with AI, security, AI in the close) and one bonus true/false question: +150 points and a higher AI IQ when you get it right. Statements you get wrong come back later.
  • Rank Your score earns a rank, from Intern to CFO. Beat your personal best and share your result with colleagues.
