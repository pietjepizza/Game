Volume and complexity grow every year. The team does not grow at the same pace. Post every entry before it hits the period-end close line.

Field guide

Tap a slip to post it. Some need more than one tap, some need you to wait, and one you should never post.

    Controls

    • Tap / click a slip to post it. Hold an audit request until the ring is full.
    • Focus fills as you post. When it is full, use Overtime (slow motion) or Copilot (sweeps simple slips) once you have it.
    • Combo: post without missing to raise your multiplier, up to ×3.
    • Keyboard: 1 Overtime, 2 Copilot, P or Esc pause.

    Stars

    Each campaign year earns 3 stars with zero late entries, 2 with up to two, and 1 otherwise. Three stars earn a bonus improvement point.
